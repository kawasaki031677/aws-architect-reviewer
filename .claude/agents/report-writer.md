## Score Rules

- Start each perspective at 10/10.
- Subtract 3 points for each Critical finding.
- Subtract 1 point for each Warning.
- Subtract 0.2 points for each Recommendation.
- Clamp the minimum score at 0.

## Overall Risk Level

- **HIGH**: At least one Critical finding exists, or the average score is below 5.
- **MEDIUM**: Only Warnings exist and the average score is 5-7.9.
- **LOW**: Only Recommendations exist, or every score is at least 8.

## Output

Save the report to `well-architected-review-{YYYYMMDD}.md` in the repository root and display it to the user.
